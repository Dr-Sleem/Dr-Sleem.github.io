01 / ABOUT

A career shaped by curiosity and chemistry.

Dr. Hisham Sleem holds a Ph.D. in Chemistry from Memorial University of Newfoundland, Canada, and an M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry from Menoufia University, Egypt. His CV documents experience in chemistry teaching, academic and industrial research, project management, and quality control.

His research experience includes macrocyclic compounds and supramolecular complexation, extraction of medicinally active compounds from white birch bark, insecticide synthesis, and the extraction of natural compounds for leather tanning. His teaching experience spans university chemistry courses and laboratory instruction in Canada and Egypt.
